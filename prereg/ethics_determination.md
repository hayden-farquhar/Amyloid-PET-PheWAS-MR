# Ethics Determination: HREC Exemption

**Project:** Phenome-wide Mendelian randomization of genetically-proxied cortical amyloid-β deposition against FinnGen R12 disease endpoints
**Investigator:** Hayden Farquhar MBBS MPHTM (ORCID 0009-0002-6226-440X)
**Date of determination:** 2026-05-19

---

## Determination

This study is **exempt from Human Research Ethics Committee (HREC) review**.

## Justification

This study uses exclusively **publicly available, aggregate-level genome-wide association study (GWAS) summary statistics**. No individual-level participant data are accessed, generated, or stored at any stage of the analysis.

### Key points

1. **No individual-level data.** All analyses operate on summary-level statistics aggregated across thousands to hundreds of thousands of participants. No individual genotype, phenotype, clinical record, or personal identifier is accessed.

2. **No re-identification risk.** GWAS summary statistics cannot be used to identify individual participants. The data contain no names, dates of birth, locations, or other personal identifiers.

3. **No intervention.** This is a secondary analysis of existing aggregate de-identified public data. No participants are recruited, contacted, or subjected to any intervention.

4. **Publicly released for research use.** All data sources are publicly released by their respective consortia for unrestricted research use, subject to acknowledgement of source consortia in publication. The intended use of these summary statistics for Mendelian randomization is consistent with the stated purposes of release by NIAGADS, EADB, FinnGen, ADNI, GTEx, eQTLGen, Biobank Japan, Pan-UKB, and the 1000 Genomes Project.

5. **No conflict with original consent terms.** Use of de-identified summary statistics is consistent with the participant consent terms under which the source studies were conducted. No re-contact, re-consent, or new participant interaction is required.

## Applicable guideline

Under the Australian National Statement on Ethical Conduct in Human Research (2007, updated 2018), research involving the use of existing, non-identifiable data that is publicly available is considered negligible-risk and is exempt from HREC review (sections 5.1.22–5.1.23).

## Record keeping

This determination is filed with the project record at OSF deposition and is referenced in the manuscript Methods section. The determination is reviewed if any aspect of the data sources changes (e.g., a source consortium revokes public access, or a new data source is added that introduces individual-level access).
